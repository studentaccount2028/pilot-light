# Pilot Light privacy policy (DRAFT, not yet reviewed by a lawyer)

*Plain-language draft for review before real residents use the service.*

**What Pilot Light does.** It watches for power outages at the home of someone who depends on powered medical equipment, counts down their backup battery, and tells the people they chose (family, a neighbor, a town volunteer, and, if they agree, emergency services) before the battery runs out.

**What we collect, and why**
- The home's address and location: so helpers and EMS can find it, and to match it to weather warnings.
- How to get in (optional): so a helper or EMS crew can reach the person quickly. This is encrypted.
- The medical devices and how long their batteries last: to run the countdown. We don't collect diagnoses.
- The resident's name, phone and a few yes/no questions (do they live alone, can they leave on their own): to decide how urgently to call for help.
- Contacts' names and phone numbers: to alert them.
- Check-ins from a phone or power module at the home: to notice when the power goes out.

**Who sees it.** The person who set up the household. During an outage, the contacts they listed. EMS, only if the household chose to share with emergency services. A town volunteer, only if the household chose to share with the town, and then without medical details in text messages. We don't sell or rent information to anyone, ever.

**Services we use.** Hosting and a database to run the app, a text-and-call service to send alerts, an email service for sign-in links, the National Weather Service for warnings, and map services for locations and drive times. They get only what they need for that job.

**Your choices.** Change or remove any information at any time. Turn off sharing with the town or EMS. Delete the household and everything about it with one button.

**Security.** Encryption, access by role, and a record of who looked at the most sensitive information. See our security overview.

**Children.** Pilot Light is not meant for use by children under 13.

**Changes and contact.** We'll post changes here and tell account holders about important ones. Questions: the contact listed in SECURITY.md.
