# Memorandum of Understanding: Pilot Light pilot (DRAFT)

**Between** [Fire Department name] ("the Department") **and** the Pilot Light team, Trevor Park and Jaiden Kim ("Pilot Light"), with [fiscal sponsor / school] as sponsor.

**Purpose.** To test whether Pilot Light helps the Department reach residents who depend on powered medical equipment during power outages, including planned Public Safety Power Shutoffs.

**Scope**
- Up to [25] households, enrolled with informed consent by the resident or their legal representative.
- Department staff receive read-only access to the EMS board through individual accounts (no shared logins). No connection to dispatch/CAD systems during the pilot.
- Duration: [3–6] months from [date].

**Pilot Light will**
- Operate the service per its security documentation, including encryption of entry information, role-based access and audit logging.
- Sign a Business Associate Agreement before any household enrolled through the Department is added, and use only providers covered by BAAs.
- Notify the Department within 24 hours of confirming any security incident involving enrolled households.
- Provide monthly pilot reports: enrollments, alerts sent, response times, false alarms, and any issues.
- Delete all pilot data within 30 days of the pilot's end unless households choose to continue.

**The Department will**
- Name a pilot lead and an IT/security contact.
- Tell Pilot Light which staff should have access and promptly report staff who leave.
- Treat Pilot Light as a supplement to, not a replacement for, 911 and existing procedures.

**Not a 911 service.** Pilot Light does not replace emergency calls. Residents and contacts are told to call 911 in an emergency.

**Costs.** No cost to the Department during the pilot.

**Termination.** Either party may end the pilot with written notice; data is then deleted as above.
